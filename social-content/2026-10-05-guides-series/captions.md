LinkedIn:
"QR code menu" gets used for two very different things.

Sometimes it means a PDF or photo of a printed menu, opened through a QR code — a static
file with no ordering behind it. Sometimes it means a live, editable menu that takes real
orders and sends them straight to the kitchen. Both get called the same name, and the
difference only shows up once you're actually running one.

We wrote up what the difference actually is, and what to check before you assume you have
